Eligibility is determined based on adjusted net family income as reported on the income tax and benefit returns of both spouses or common-law partners for the base taxation year. Failure to file returns by the applicable deadline of April 30 may result in the suspension of benefit entitlements.

Families who received payments last year will be reassessed automatically. Recipients are advised to verify that direct deposit information remains current, as outdated banking details may delay disbursement.
