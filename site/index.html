New

Helpers that feel like magic.

Each one works on the demo website above. In Chrome, the AI runs on your own computer.

  • Explain simply AI

    Select hard text. Get 3 short points, in the way that fits you. "Even simpler" goes further.

    Or select any text in the demo website, then press the button that appears.

  • Show me how AI

    Type what you want to do. A pointer glides to the right button and says "Click here", step by step. It works in web apps like Word online too. It never clicks "buy" or "send" for you.

    A kind daily limit for sites like Instagram. No shame. One "5 more minutes" a day.

Who Ease is for

  • ADHD and focus

    A reading line, calm pages without moving ads, kind time limits and short explanations.

  • Dyslexia

    Clear fonts (including OpenDyslexic and Atkinson Hyperlegible), more space, and read aloud with word highlighting.

  • Autism and sensory overload

    Pauses animations and autoplay videos, softens bright colours, limits loud volume, and blurs words you choose.

  • Low vision

    Bigger text, dark or high contrast, a big cursor, a strong focus ring and big tooltips for icons.

  • Tremor, Parkinson's and arthritis

    Ignores clicks made twice by mistake, makes small links easier to hit, and adds big scroll buttons.

  • Older adults

    Bigger text, clear links, no pop-ups, names under icon buttons, Show me how, and scam warnings.

  • Scam safety

    Warns about look-alike websites (like paypa1.com), fake virus pages and password boxes on unsafe pages.

  • Anxiety and stress

    Hides fake pressure like "Only 2 left!" and countdown timers, and keeps pages calm.

Questions

Is Ease free?

Yes. Ease is free. There are no ads and no paid version.

Is the demo the real extension?

Yes. The demo loads the same code as the Chrome extension. Only the settings are kept in this page, not in Chrome. If your browser has no built-in AI, Explain simply shows a recorded example.

Do I need an account?

No. Ease has no accounts and no server. Your settings stay on your computer.

Will Ease break websites?

We test Ease on many busy websites. If a page looks wrong, turn Ease off for that site only.

Which languages does Ease support?

English, French, Spanish, Italian, Hindi, Punjabi, Arabic, Tagalog, Simplified Chinese and Traditional Chinese (Hong Kong).

Ease is coming to the Chrome Web Store soon. Until then, you can install it in about one minute.

Download for Chrome · version 0.24.0See the code on GitHub

  1. Unzip the file you downloaded.
  2. In Chrome, open chrome://extensions.
  3. Turn on Developer mode (top right).
  4. Click Load unpacked, and choose the unzipped folder.

The setup page opens by itself. It asks a few questions and turns on the right profiles for you.
